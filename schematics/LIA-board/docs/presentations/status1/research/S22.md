## Options (decision topics only)
- Option symmetric drain layout (keep both drain paths strictly symmetric): Risk: leakage scales with the capacitance mismatch `ΔC` (S22-F02), and the `44 dB` figure assumes a `±10 %` mismatch (S22-F03); achieved symmetry is not in the sources (S22-NF06).
- Option on-board CM calibration (subtract the static, mostly quadrature leakage): Risk: the subtraction is only claimed for the "mostly quadrature (Y) and static" part (S22-F04), and no residual result exists (S22-NF07); the calibration position's presence in the as-drawn circuit is unverified (S22-F13, S22-NF09).
- Option re-simulation SIM-12 (System CMRR with real node capacitance, ≥ 200 MC runs, median ≥ 48 dB): Risk: SIM-12 has no result yet (S22-F09); its `C_node` (50 pF) conflicts with item 10's 60 pF (S22-C02) and it still includes a mux that D-19 removed (S22-C03).
- Option measurement LAB-6 / LAB-10 (bench check of the leakage): Risk: the LAB procedures and status are not in the listed files (S22-NF03); item 10 names them as the measurement path (S22-F05).
