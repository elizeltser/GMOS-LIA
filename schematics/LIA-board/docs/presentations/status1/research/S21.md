## Options (decision topic)
- Option "Keep `TTL-232R-3V3`, confirm RXD accepts `5 V` logic": the cable's `5 V` VCC is kept, and the cable's RXD tolerance is confirmed. Risk: the RXD tolerance is not in the sources, so the option is unverified, cites S21-F06, S21-F07(a), S21-NF02.
- Option "3.3 V LDO on the isolated side": a regulator is added after the cable's `5 V` VCC. Risk: the isolated side's only ground return named in the sources is the cable's GND pin, and LDO current and dropout are not in the sources, cites S21-F07(b), S21-F09, S21-F11, S21-NF05.
- Option "Specify `TTL-232R-5V`": the cable is changed to the 5 V variant. Risk: its logic levels, pin order and availability are not in the sources, and DG-4 fixes the header to TTL-232R pin order, cites S21-F07(c), S21-F08, S21-NF03.
- Option "5 V-tolerant parts on the isolated side" (named in D-21 as an alternative to the LDO, not further specified): Risk: the parts and their tolerance are not named in the sources, and the `ISO6721` supply range is not in the sources, cites S21-F05, S21-NF04.
- Chosen approach as a whole (`ISO6721` + external cable): Risk: the D-21 status is open and the cable VCC handling is unresolved, cites S21-F02, S21-F05.
- Rejected alternative "On-board CP2102N + USB": Risk: no assessment or rejection reason is recorded in the sources, cites S21-F03, S21-F04.
- Rejected alternative "ISOUSB211 + MCU USB": Risk: no assessment or rejection reason is recorded in the sources, cites S21-F03, S21-F04.
