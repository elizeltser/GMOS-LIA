## Options (decision topics only)
- Option P0 (as drawn): PD 12 V, two `LMR51430` (two converters: +7 V buck, −7 V IBB). Risk: noise grade `B−` with two free-running clocks and no SYNC pin, so the beat can only be filtered; IBB IC ground sits at −7 V; area grade `C+`. Cites S20-F15, S20-F37, S20-F38, S20-F43.
- Option P1 (one `TPS65130`, USB-C 5 V, no PD): one IC for both rails, one clock, +7 V and −7 V with 820 Ω preload. Risk: changes DC-1, DC-2, DG-9, D-10 and D-14, needs new input-protection and source current-class requirements, and the source may be Default-USB-only (100 mA) against 0.26–0.34 A draw. Requirement fit C. Cites S20-F16, S20-F33, S20-F34, S20-F35. Under FE-C it becomes a 5 V boost (S20-F47).
- Option P2 (one `LMR51430` + `LTC3260` charge pump + LT3094): removes U18 and L2; two clocks; the LTC3260 must run at fixed 500 kHz. Risk: noise grade `B`, cost grade `C` (cost-based), four extended parts, and the LTC3260's own LDO is not recommended (noise). Cites S20-F24, S20-F37, S20-F44, S20-F53.
- Option P3 (bench ±9 V only, no switcher): LDOs only. Risk: not stand-alone; loses DC-1 and the USB input; requirement fit D. Cites S20-F25, S20-F35, S20-F37.
- Option P4 (P1 plus bench ±9 V through 0 Ω links): stand-alone noise `B+` (`A` on the bench) and first-spin A/B measurement. Risk: requirement fit B (stand-alone on USB 5 V only) and the same USB source-current issue as P1. Cites S20-F26, S20-F35, S20-F37, S20-F34.
- Option P5 (one `LMR51430` + diode-capacitor pump on the SW node, about −10.5 V, then LT3094): one clock; smallest change to the drawn board; requirement fit A. Risk: the −rail is unregulated and depends on buck load, the pump ripple (about 100 mV pk-pk) is unmeasured, and +7 V and −7 V start and stop together. Under FE-C it reduces to P0 without U18. Cites S20-F27, S20-F28, S20-F29, S20-F30, S20-F35.
- Option P6 (two bucks with SYNC inputs, one MCU timer; not graded): would remove the beat, but the LMR51430 has no SYNC pin and no parts are selected. Risk: unknown, not costed, not graded. Cites S20-F07, S20-F32.

Study recommendation (conditional, §6.1): P1 with TPS65130 if the 5 V USB input is acceptable (OQ-3, OQ-4); P4 for the first spin; P5 if the 12 V PD input must stay. Cites S20-F45.

Front-end effect (D-18): FE-A and FE-B are graded the same (S20-F46). The study's light-load DCM risk applies to FE-B (S20-F51). FE-C removes the −rail, so P0, P2 and P5 become "PD + one buck" and P1 becomes a 5 V boost (S20-F47, S20-F48). Owner recommends FE-B (S20-F50). The study does not close D-18 (S20-F49).
