## Options (decision topics only)
- Option A (MCU on the board in spin 1): Risk: every MCU-side fault (pins, XO, timer and DMA chain, DAC/ADC drivers, VDDA) enters the same first bring-up as the analog chain. The MCU sheet is still open (T08 unchecked; PA4, PC6/PC7, PH0 unconnected; no XO or decoupling), firmware FW-11…FW-16 are outlined only, and the drain-sense ADC channel is missing from the firmware spec. Cites S18-F21, S18-F28, S18-F29, S18-C04.
- Option B (analog-only board in spin 1, MCU on an external EVB via jumpers or cable): Risk: the sine (`EXC_DAC`), both references (`REF_X` / `REF_Y`) and the ADC and DAC lines would cross jumpers, and the timer link that keeps sine and references phase-locked stays inside one MCU. VDDA would come from the EVB, so the board alone does not meet DC-4c and AN-7. The PC link still depends on the open D-21 cable VCC question. Cites S18-F02, S18-F03, S18-F13, S18-F36, S18-F37, S18-F10. Precedent: the Pace V2 board (S18-F31, S18-F32) used headers and held its ADC unpowered until firmware was ready. No outcome is recorded (S18-NF04).
- Option C (no third option in the docs; closest documented item: keep the MCU on the board and add test points for an external SR860 as fallback demodulator): Risk: the SR860 fallback only replaces the demodulator; it still needs the MCU's gate and reference signals at the test points, so it does not remove the MCU dependency. Cites S18-F16, S18-NF01.
