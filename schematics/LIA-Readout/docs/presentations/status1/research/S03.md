## Conflicts (report, do not resolve)
- S03-C01: Supply. `simulations/design-context.md` §2 says `Single rail, 0 V to 5 V, for every part` (Current) and its note says it is "still not sure if we use single rail or +-5V". `requirements.md` DC-1 has a `12 V` PD input, and DC-4 has `+5VA` plus `−5VA` only if FE-A or FE-B. `README.md` §9.2 item 8 and `design-issues.md` DI-10 refer to `±5 V`. Owner per DI-10: the user decides.
- S03-C02: In-amp gain. `design-context.md` §4 says `11 V/V`, `RG = 5 kOhm (C-02)` (Current). `requirements.md` AN-20 says `G = 10`. No owner row in `README.md` §9.2; DI-14 uses G = 11 in its simulation.
- S03-C03: Excitation waveform. `design-context.md` §1 says `Square wave synchronous with the reference` (Draft). `requirements.md` AN-2 and FW-1 say a `sine` from DAC1 filtered by the gate stages, with square-wave references. Logged in `README.md` §9.1 D-3 and D-22. No owner row in §9.2.
- S03-C04: ADC. `design-context.md` §1 says `ADC type not chosen (TBD)`, and §9 asks "Which ADC". `requirements.md` AN-30 chooses ADS1220 (`24-bit ΔΣ`). DI-13 analyses 16/18/20/24-bit 5 V converters.
- S03-C05: Output time constant. `design-context.md` §5 says `tau = 0.1 s at present` (Current) and §6 says `10 kOhm and 10 uF` (Current). Derived: `10 kOhm × 10 uF = 0.1 s`. `requirements.md` AN-26 says `τ = 500 ms` (f_c = 0.318 Hz), and `README.md` §9.1 D-9 says `τ = 0.5 s`.
- S03-C06: AD630. `design-context.md` §8 excludes `AD630` (Current, "unless their stock has changed"). `README.md` §9.1 D-18 (FE-A, as drawn) and `requirements.md` AN-24 use an AD630 pair. Owner: D-18 decision.
- S03-C07: Frequency range. `design-context.md` §3 says `100 Hz to 10 kHz` (Draft). `requirements.md` AN-1 says `≈ 400–700 Hz` and AN-21 says `377–711 Hz`. DI-16 is Minor and open.

## Options (decision topics only)
- Option FE-A: AD630 ±2 on `±5 V`, as drawn. Risk: AD630 is single-source and not stocked at LCSC/JLC; needs `−5VA` and `−7 V` rails. Cites S03-F01, S03-F04, S03-F07.
- Option FE-B: TMUX6136 + OPA2196 switch demodulator on `±5 V`. Risk: TMUX6136 supply margin is inadequate (`Critical`); the replacement is TMUX1136DGSR. Needs `−5VA` and `−7 V`. Cites S03-F01, S03-F04, S03-F08, S03-F09. Owner's recommendation (context above); formal closure in `README.md` §9.1 pending.
- Option FE-C: single 5 V, V_REF 2.5 V, INA333 + LM7705, 2× TMUX1219, OPA2365. Risk: OPA2196 input transition region ≈ `2.0–3.5 V` on 5 V (`README.md` §9.2 item 13), so the OPA2365 replaces it; needs `V_REF` (DC-4d). Cites S03-F01, S03-F04.
- Decision: supply architecture (one rail or `±5 V`). Risk: front-end page in Capture cannot be finalised. Cites S03-F05, S03-C01.
- Decision: VDDA source (D-D). Risk: excitation amplitude stability (AN-7). Cites S03-F06, S03-C09.
- Decision: band width of the band-pass (fixed or wider or switchable). Risk: frequency programmability (AN-1, AN-21). Cites S03-F13, S03-F14, S03-C07.
- Decision: UART cable VCC handling (3.3 V LDO or 5 V-tolerant parts). Risk: isolated-side power. Cites S03-F24.
