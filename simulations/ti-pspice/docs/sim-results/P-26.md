## Method

**P26a.** The AC grid has 100 points per decade, so 518 Hz and 65.786 kHz are not grid points. The values are interpolated: log|H| and the unwrapped phase are linear in log f between the two neighbouring grid points (512.861 Hz and 524.807 Hz at 518 Hz). The interpolation error of the phase is estimated from a quadratic through three neighbours, and it is -0.00055 deg. The transfer function is V(GATE) divided by acexc = 1 V. The reference is the closed form `mfb()` of `docs/sim-results/scripts/afe_eval_expected.py`, which assumes an ideal op amp.

**P26b.** The window is the last 40 whole periods of 518 Hz, from 0.1 s minus 40 T to 0.1 s (77.22 ms). The node voltages are resampled to a uniform 0.5 us grid by linear interpolation. A least-squares fit gives the DC level, sine and cosine at k f0 (k = 1 to 10), and at the image frequencies 127 f0, 129 f0, 255 f0 and 257 f0 (the ZOH images of the 128-sample staircase). With whole periods in the window, the fitted amplitudes are exact. Phases refer to t = 0, so an ideal sin(w t) has phase 0.

**Edge check (P26b).** Each staircase edge at k ts and at k ts + 100 ns must have a solver time point within 5 ns, where ts = 1 / (128 f0) = 15.08 us. The check uses the first 4000 edges of the window. The median solver step is 128.0 ns, and 100 % of the edges pass.

**ZOH phase (P26b).** The staircase holds each sample over [k ts, (k+1) ts]. This delays the fundamental by ts/2 = 7.54 us, which is 1.40625 deg of lag at 518 Hz. The 100 ns linear edges add a lag of 50 ns, which is 0.00932 deg. The expected fundamental phase of EXC_DAC is therefore -1.4156 deg (sign: lag is negative). The board phase of the gate follows from the same convention.

**P26c.** The step is the mean in 3.5 to 4.0 ms minus the mean in 0.9 to 1.0 ms. The overshoot is (V_final - V_min) / |dV|, with V_min the sample minimum after the step. A damped cosine with an offset is fitted to 1.0 to 3.9 ms. The fit gives the damped frequency fd, the decay rate a, the damping ratio zeta = a / w0, the undamped frequency f0 = sqrt(fd^2 + (a / 2 pi)^2) and Q = 1 / (2 zeta). The closed form uses f0 = 1 / (2 pi sqrt(R2 R3 C1 C2)) = 4949.5 Hz, Q = sqrt(C1 / C2) sqrt(R2 R3) / (R2 + R3 + R2 R3 / R1) = 0.7278, and overshoot exp(-pi zeta / sqrt(1 - zeta^2)) = 5.129 % with zeta = 1 / (2 Q).

**Settling.** For P26b, the fundamental amplitude and phase are compared between the first and the second half of the window (20 periods each). For P26c, the final level is compared between the first and the second half of the final window (0.25 ms each). For P26a, no settling applies because the analysis is in the frequency domain.

**Analyst limits.** The entry sets some limits. The others are the analyst's choice and are marked "analyst" in the comparison table. The owner should confirm them.

## Discussion

**MFB magnitude and phase.** The magnitude at 518 Hz differs from the closed form by +0.0097 %. The phase differs by -0.030 deg. The interpolation error is -0.00055 deg, so most of the phase difference is real. A single-pole op amp with GBW 1.1 MHz explains it: the nodal model of the MFB gives +0.0071 % in magnitude and -0.028 deg in phase. The GBW of 1.1 MHz is an estimate, not a verified value of the OPAx196 model. A GBW of 2.5 MHz gives -0.012 deg. The interpolated phase agrees with the model within 0.002 deg.

**Gate phase change of the 1 nF load.** The entry gives 0.019 deg. This value is R59 times 1 nF times omega, which is 0.0186 deg. It is the effect of 1 nF alone. The change from 50 pF to 1 nF is R59 times (1 nF minus 50 pF) times omega, which is 0.0177 deg. The simulation gives -0.0177 deg for this step, which matches the closed form. The sign is negative because the larger load adds lag. The entry should quote 0.018 deg for the step, or 0.019 deg for 1 nF alone.

**DAC image of the MFB (model deviation).** The ideal MFB gives -44.945 dB at 65.786 kHz. The simulated MFB gives -40.910 dB for 50 pF and -40.808 dB for 1 nF. The simulated gain is 4.04 dB above the closed form. The excess grows with frequency: +0.48 dB at 20 kHz, +4.06 dB at 66 kHz, +7.4 dB at 100 kHz and about +34 dB at 1 MHz. The simulated response falls only about 20 dB per decade above 100 kHz. The ideal MFB falls faster.

A finite GBW alone does not explain this. A single-pole op amp with GBW 1.1 MHz gives -45.02 dB at 65.786 kHz, and GBW 2.5 MHz gives -44.97 dB. Both are slightly lower than the ideal value. An output resistance Ro in series with the op-amp output does raise the image attenuation. With GBW 1.1 MHz, Ro = 200 Ohm gives -43.31 dB, Ro = 300 Ohm gives -42.57 dB and Ro = 500 Ohm gives -41.27 dB. A single Ro does not fit the whole range. With Ro = 500 Ohm the model minus simulation is -0.5 dB at 100 kHz, +0.3 dB at 302 kHz and +1.3 dB at 1 MHz. The GBW and Ro values are not verified against the OPAx196 model. The most likely cause is feedthrough from NC7 through R5 into the op-amp output impedance. This is not tested here.

Against the analyst limit of 1 dB this is a model deviation, not a failure of the requirement. The gate image result does not depend on it, because the measured gate image of -83.28 dBc agrees with the simulated MFB (see the image rows below).

**Excitation image and gate image.** The ZOH image of the 128-sample staircase is -42.08 dBc before the filter, as measured at EXC_DAC. The measured image at the gate is -83.28 dBc. The expected value with the simulated MFB is -42.08 dBc - 40.910 dB = -82.99 dBc. The difference is -0.29 dB. The value with the ideal MFB, -87.0 dBc, is not the relevant comparison, because the simulated MFB is what the gate sees. The gate image passes the entry limit of -40 dBc with 43 dB of margin. It passes the limit of -80 dBc with 3.3 dB of margin.

**Gate THD and harmonics.** The THD of the gate is 0.027 %, far below the 0.3 % limit. The EXC_DAC harmonics depend on the resampling grid: h3 is 48.49 uV on the 0.5 us grid, 48.62 uV on a 0.1 us grid, and 48.72 uV from the exact code DFT times the ZOH and edge sincs. The matching h5 values are 63.40, 64.03 and 63.97 uV. With the exact values the expected gate harmonics are 48.72 / 119.92 = 0.406 uV (h3) and 63.97 / 122.63 = 0.522 uV (h5). The measured 0.357 uV and 0.540 uV differ by -0.049 uV and +0.018 uV. Both differences are within the 0.31 uV rms fit residual of the gate.

**Gate amplitude.** The gate amplitude follows from the excitation. The EXC_DAC fundamental is 299.92129 mV, and 2 x 299.92129 mV / 119.92188 = 5.00194 mVpp. The fitted gate amplitude is 5.00194 mVpp, a difference of 0.0000 %. The 300 mV staircase gives a fundamental of 299.92129 mV because of the ZOH sinc and the 12-bit codes.

**ZOH phase.** The entry says that "the ZOH adds a half-sample delay of 1.41 deg to the phase of P26a". This can be read as "the board phase equals the P26a phase plus the ZOH delay", which is consistent with the simulation. It is not an error of the entry, but it should be stated more clearly. The measured fundamental of EXC_DAC lags the ideal sine by 1.4155 deg. The expected lag is 1.406 deg from the half-sample hold (ts/2 = 7.54 us, which is 1.40625 deg at 518 Hz), plus 0.0093 deg from the 50 ns edges. The total expected lag is 1.4156 deg, and the measured value agrees within 0.0001 deg. The sign convention is that a lag is negative. The gate minus EXC_DAC phase is +171.7005 deg, which agrees with P26a (+171.6972 deg) within 0.0033 deg.

**Step response and ringing.** The gate step is -2.49999 mV, equal to the exact closed form -0.3 V x (-1/120) = -2.50000 mV. The overshoot is 5.246 % from the sample minimum and 5.229 % from the damped-sinusoid fit. The closed form gives 5.129 % for Q = 0.7278. The overshoot is 0.10 to 0.12 percentage points above the closed form, which is a 2 % relative difference. The fitted damping ratio is 0.6847 and the fitted Q is 0.7303, which differ from the closed form by 0.3 %. The fitted damped frequency is 3610 Hz, compared with 3597 Hz from the closed form (+0.39 %). This is a second-order ringing of the MFB, not an instability of U1B. The overshoot does not change between cgate = 50 pF and 1 nF, so the gate load does not reduce the damping at these values. No ringing above 100 kHz is seen. The P26a grid is monotonic above 100 kHz, and the P26c fit residual is 0.11 to 0.13 uV rms against a 3.45 mV ringing.

**Settling.** The fundamental of the gate in the P26b window is 2.50097 mV in both halves (0.0000 % difference), and the EXC_DAC fundamental differs by 0.0009 %. The P26c final level differs between the two halves of its window by 0.00037 % of the step. All three settling checks pass the analyst limit of 0.1 %.

**Edge resolution.** The 2 us maximum step of P26b does resolve the staircase. The median solver step in the window is 128 ns. Every one of the first 4000 staircase edges has a solver time point within 5 ns. The P26b fit with the ZOH images included gives the same fundamental, phase and image as an analytic 12-bit staircase with 100 ns edges (299.9213 mV, -1.4155 deg, -42.08 dBc). The fit residual of 1.45 mV rms is the ZOH images above 257 f0, which the basis does not include. Adding them step by step lowers the residual to 1.076 mV rms, and the analytic staircase gives the same values.

## Open points

1. The simulated MFB image is 4.04 dB above the ideal closed form. The most likely cause is the output impedance of the op amp and feedthrough from NC7 through R5 (GBW 1.1 MHz and Ro 200 to 500 Ohm give -41 to -43 dB). Neither value is verified against the OPAx196 model. A run with a separate op-amp model, or with R5 and C6 changed, would test it. The gate image result does not depend on this.
2. The entry's MFB image value (-44.9 dB) and gate image value (-87.0 dBc with the ideal MFB) are ideal values. The requirement uses the simulated gate, which is -83.0 dBc with the simulated MFB. The owner should state which value is the requirement.
3. The entry's 0.019 deg for the 1 nF load is the 1 nF value alone. The step from 50 pF to 1 nF is 0.0177 deg, which the simulation gives.
4. The entry's ZOH sentence can be read as "board phase equals P26a plus the ZOH delay". The owner should state the sign convention once, as in this summary.
5. The analyst limits in the Method table are not all from the entry. The owner should confirm them.
6. The transient gate image is 0.29 dB below the AC prediction from P26a (-41.20 dB against -40.91 dB, relative to the EXC_DAC image). Fit noise explains about 0.06 dB. The cause of the other 0.23 dB is not verified. It may be the staircase sampling or the transient step of the MFB.
7. The phase margin of U1B at 100 pF and 1 nF (79 deg and 55 deg, from the 2026-10-29 review) is not tested directly. The P26c step shows a second-order ringing at 3.61 kHz and no ringing above 100 kHz, which is consistent with a large phase margin, but this is an indirect check.
