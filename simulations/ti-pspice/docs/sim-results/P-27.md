# P-27 Full chain: scale factor and phase over a full turn

## Goal

Measure the X/Y transfer of the whole LIA-AFE-Eval board from the gate to X_OUT and Y_OUT, with vendor models, over the excitation phase. Locate the remainder of about -0.7 deg of the 7 November 2026 smoke run and the judge note of P-27.

## Method

**Time step.** The transient has a maximum step of 5 us. At 518 Hz this gives 386.1 points per period. The largest solver step in the analysed windows is 5.046 us, which gives 382.5 points per period. The fit is therefore on about 380 points per period.

**Window.** The means of X_OUT and Y_OUT are taken over 20 whole periods of 518 Hz (38.61 ms) from 60 ms, so the window runs from 60.00 ms to 98.61 ms.

**Phase fit.** Each probed node is resampled to a uniform 1 us grid in the same window. A least-squares fit of c + a sin(w t) + b cos(w t) at 518 Hz gives the complex phasor C = a + j b = A exp(j phi), with phi the phase of A sin(w t + phi). The reference is EXC_DAC = 1.65 V + 0.3 V sin(w t + pexc), so its phase is pexc. The 2f0 content is orthogonal to the fit over whole periods.

**Stage phases.** Each stage is the ratio of two phasors. The drain difference is DRN_B - DRN_A. The chain is GATE / EXC, DRN_A / GATE, (DRN_B - DRN_A) / GATE, INA_OUT / (DRN_B - DRN_A), HPF_OUT / INA_OUT, BPF_OUT / HPF_OUT and BPF_OUT / (DRN_B - DRN_A).

**X/Y.** R = sqrt(X_OUT^2 + Y_OUT^2) and phi_XY = atan2(-Y_OUT, X_OUT) are compared with the phase of BPF_OUT.

**Settling.** Two whole-period windows of 10 periods each are used, from 60 ms and from 60 ms + 10 T0 (19.31 ms each). The difference of their means is the settling trend. The analytic settling bias of the critically damped post filter (tau 3 ms) at 60 ms is (1 + t/tau) exp(-t/tau) = 21 exp(-20) = 4.3e-8 of the initial error, which is negligible.

**Drain node model.** The pixel is an ideal current source on side A. The two drain nodes form an admittance matrix [[Y, -jw Cd], [-jw Cd, Y]] with Y = 1/R_D + jw C_self. The normalised transfer T = R_D V / I is 1 at DC. Two cases are given. In the 60 pF case C_self = cnode + cmux = 50 pF + 10 pF and Cd = 0. In the 71 pF case C_self = 60 pF + C_CMN (10 pF) + C_DIFF (1 pF) and Cd = 1 pF, where the INA828 values come from `INA828.LIB`.

**Expected values.** The closed forms use ideal op amps. The INA828, HPF and LPF stage values are the P-24 vendor deficits at 517.571 Hz (-0.059 deg, -0.055 deg, -0.062 deg). The MFB value is the P-26 vendor phase (171.6972 deg). The gate amplitude is 0.3 V divided by the MFB gain magnitude.

## Discussion

**Scale factor and orthogonality.** The measured R is constant over the turn to 0.054 %. The phase relation phi - pexc is constant to 0.0026 deg. This shows the X/Y orthogonality of the chain, which is the AN-25 check of the entry. The pass criteria of the entry are met.

**Scale factor split.** The scale factor is R / |v_drain|. The pixel and R_D loss in the drain stage (the R_D || C amplitude loss of about 0.29 %) cancels in this ratio, because the same loss is in both the drain and R. The drain stage therefore adds only the feedthrough to DRN_B. The split of the measured -0.033 % against the ideal 25.2020 V/V is:

| Part | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Front-end vendor deficit (39.5785 against 39.5872 V/V) | -0.022 % | P-24 vendor deficit |
| Feedthrough (\|DRN_B - DRN_A\| against \|DRN_A\|) | -0.008 % | 71 pF model, two-node |
| Demodulator (R against 2/pi \|BPF_OUT\|) | -0.003 % | this run |
| Sum | -0.033 % | measured 25.1937 V/V against ideal 25.2020 V/V |

The scale factor uses \|DRN_A\|. Against \|DRN_B - DRN_A\| it is 25.1958 V/V. The entry's E-9 value of 25.5 V/V is rounded. The exact value at G = 10 is 2/pi x 4 x 10 = 25.465 V/V. At G = 9.8968 this gives 25.202 V/V, which is the ideal value of this entry. The measured value is therefore -0.033 % against E-9 scaled to the board gain. E-9 has no tolerance in the entry, so this is reported and not a pass criterion.

**Location of the -0.7 deg.** The stage table splits the phase of the chain at 518 Hz. The MFB, the front end and the INA828 stage agree with the closed forms and the P-24 and P-26 vendor values within 0.006 deg each. The whole deficit sits in the drain stage. Its amplitude is 0.083 % low and its lag is 0.68 deg high. Both values match one node capacitance of 71 pF, that is 11 pF more than the 60 pF of the entry.

**Source of the extra 11 pF.** The INA828 vendor model (`INA828.LIB`, from `nom_pspti.lib`) connects 10 pF from each input pin to MID (C_CMN and C_CMP). Its differential input capacitance C_DIFF is 1 pF between the two input pins. The drain node DRN_A is the IN- pin, so it sees 10 pF in the model. DRN_B is the IN+ pin, with 10 pF as well. The 1 pF couples DRN_A into DRN_B. Because DRN_B has no pixel drive, its voltage comes only from this coupling. The two-node model gives DRN_B / DRN_A = 0.10709 % at +85.639 deg. The simulated DRN_B amplitude of 0.01069 mV matches this ratio. The effective node capacitance seen by DRN_A is 60 + 10 + 1 = 71 pF, because DRN_B is close to zero AC.

**Model check.** The two-node model at 71 pF gives DRN_A |H| 0.997104 with a lag of 4.3608 deg. The difference (DRN_B - DRN_A) has |H| 0.997023 at a phase of -4.4220 deg, of which -0.0612 deg is the feedthrough. The whole chain is then 171.6972 - 4.4220 + 0.0141 - 0.1770 = pexc + 167.1123 deg, against the measured pexc + 167.1138 deg. The residual is +0.0015 deg.

**The entry prediction.** The judge note assumed 60 pF and the closed-form MFB. Its prediction of pexc + 167.87 deg therefore omits the 11 pF of the INA828 model, the B-side feedthrough and the vendor MFB phase. With the 60 pF two-node model and the vendor MFB the prediction is pexc + 167.847 deg. The judge note value (167.87 deg) differs from this only by the closed-form MFB and the rounding of the R_D lag.

**Effect on the design.** The drain lag is constant over the turn (spread 0.0026 deg). It therefore shifts the phase calibration only. It does not change the X/Y orthogonality. The 0.08 % amplitude loss of the drain stage (R_D with the extra capacitance) applies to the gate-to-output gain. It does not apply to the scale factor X_OUT / v_drain, because R and v_drain lose the same amount.

**Settling.** The 10-period windows (19.31 ms each) are whole periods of 518 Hz, and so of 2f0 as well. The 2f0 ripple of the post filter averages to zero in both windows. The measured trend is 0.00078 mV in X_OUT and 0.00050 mV in Y_OUT. This is below the 0.0011 mV bound of the judge note.

## Open points

1. The 10 pF and 1 pF input capacitances are read from `INA828.LIB`. A separate run with the INA828 input capacitances removed (for example, a copy of the model with C_CMN, C_CMP and C_DIFF set to zero) would confirm the location directly. This run was not made. The 71 pF conclusion rests on the stage data, which match it within 0.006 deg and 0.01 %.
2. Closed by the main session (7 November 2026): the R prediction with the closed-form MFB and 71 pF must use the differential drain factor 0.997023, not the single-ended DRN_A factor 0.997104 (9.97656 mV). With 9.97573 mV it is 251.347 mV, and the measured 251.366 mV is 0.007 % above it. The script line that prints 251.368 mV uses the single-ended factor.
3. Check of the design values. `cnode` of about 50 pF agrees with `simulations/design-context.md` (drain path row, "drain node capacitance about 50 pF ± 10 %", status Current). The value `cmux = 10 pF` is not in `design-context.md`. It is the TMUX1109 assumption of `afe_eval.cir` (on-path capacitance per side). The INA828 input capacitance of 10 pF is a vendor model value, not a design value. Whether the 50 pF of `design-context.md` already includes cmux and the INA828 input capacitance is not stated there. The judge or the user should decide this, and the analyst does not change `design-context.md`.
4. The E-9 value is 25.5 V/V at G = 10, as quoted by the entry. The source of E-9 is not checked here.
5. The gate phase with the vendor MFB (171.6972 deg, P-26) differs from the closed form (171.7274 deg) by -0.030 deg. This is the vendor MFB effect of P-26. The entry used the closed form.
6. The judge note values of the drain amplitude (9.9848 mV) and the phase (pexc + 167.87 deg) are superseded by this run. The entry should be updated by the judge, not by the analyst.
