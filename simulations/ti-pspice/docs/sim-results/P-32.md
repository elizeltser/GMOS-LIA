# P-32 Overload and DC step at the drains

## Goal

Measure the recovery after a large drain signal and after a step of the drain DC (DAC trim, pixel switching).

The circuit copies are `circuits/afe_eval_P32a.cir` and `circuits/afe_eval_P32b.cir`. Both are `circuits/afe_eval.cir` with `cpf = 15n` on the `.PARAM` line. The post-filter is then a two-pole unity-gain Sallen-Key with 200 kOhm and 15 nF per pole (tau 3 ms per pole, Q = 0.5). The board value cpf = 1.5 uF gives 0.3 s per pole. P32b adds `V_DSTEP DAC2 DAC2S PULSE(0 {dstep} 50m 1u 1u 1 2)` in series with `V_DAC2`, which now sits on `DAC2S`, and `.PARAM dstep=0`.

The analysis script is `docs/sim-results/scripts/P-32_overload.py`. Run it from `simulations/ti-pspice` with `python docs/sim-results/scripts/P-32_overload.py`. Its last part (`part_c`, added at grading on 8 November 2026) computes the phase, the slew model, the cross-check cause and the recovery model below.

## Method

**Window.** The results use the last 20 whole periods at f0 = 518 Hz (0.2614 s to 0.3 s). Amplitudes come from a least-squares fit at f0 over this window. Means of X_OUT and Y_OUT are time averages over the same window. The window of 20 periods from 60 ms still holds a start-up transient in BPF_OUT. The two windows agree to 0.014 mV for the X_OUT and Y_OUT means and to 0.011 % for the BPF_OUT amplitude.

**Fit.** The fit uses a uniform 1 us grid obtained by linear interpolation of the adaptive samples. A fit on the raw samples is biased by 0.4 % (`docs/simulation-quirks.md`).

**Time step.** The largest sample step after 60 ms is 2.019 us in P32a and 2.013 us in P32b, against the 2 us maximum of the `.TRAN` line.

**Drain difference.** The drain step of P-32b is measured as the difference from the dstep = 0 run on the same 1 us grid. The difference is then averaged over one period (a trailing boxcar of length T0 = 1.93 ms). This removes the f0 and 2f0 ripple. The raw difference is reported too.

**Recovery.** The recovery time is the last time after the step at 50 ms at which the period-averaged difference is at least 0.1 mV, measured from 50 ms. The trailing boxcar adds a lag of about T0/2 = 0.97 ms. A centred boxcar gives 1.0 ms shorter times (13.4 ms to 22.5 ms).

**Cross-check.** The dstep = 0 run of P32b and the vda = 10 mV run of P32a have the same element values, but P32b has the extra source V_DSTEP (0 V) and its node DAC2S. Their maximum differences over 60 ms to 0.3 s are X_OUT 0.013 mV, Y_OUT 0.063 mV, INA_OUT 0.021 mV and BPF_OUT 2.6 mV. The cause is given in the Discussion.

**Measured drain.** The drain amplitude of the pixel stand-in is 9.977 mV at the nominal vda = 10 mV (fit, P32a). The entry values assume exactly 10 mV, so the drain-based checks use the measured amplitude.

## Discussion

**Gain and linearity.** The chain up to BPF_OUT is linear in gain to 0.01 % up to 100 mV drain amplitude. BPF_OUT over the measured drain is 39.571 V/V. P-27 gives 39.5785 V/V, so the two agree to 0.02 %. The entry value of 1.979 V at 50 mV is correct for an exact 50 mV drain, but the simulation drain is 49.884 mV at the nominal setting. The entry's 3.96 V at 100 mV is also correct for an exact 100 mV drain.

**Y_OUT error is a phase rotation in the demodulator.** The phase of (X, Y) changes by -0.044 deg at 50 mV and -0.099 deg at 100 mV, while the phase of BPF_OUT changes by only -0.005 deg. The rotation therefore arises in the demodulator, not in the filters. Y_OUT is the small component here (Y/X = 0.228, phase 12.87 deg from the X axis). A rotation of -0.099 deg changes Y_OUT by -0.78 % but R by only -0.022 %. A Y error alone is thus not a meaningful linearity measure, and the design-context threshold is applied to R and to the phase instead.

The mechanism is the slew rate of U27A at the Y reference edges. U27A gives Y_DEM = 2 D2 - BPF_OUT. The Y edges fall near the BPF_OUT peaks (BPF_OUT = 0.975 A at each edge), so Y_DEM steps by 2 x 0.975 A, which is 7.70 V at 100 mV. The script shows that both Y edges of each period step Y_DEM downwards. The falling slew rate therefore applies. In OPAx196.LIB (stage AOL_2, INEG = -5.24 A into C_C5 = 790 nF) the falling rate is 6.63 V/us, and the rising rate (IPOS = 7.07 A) is 8.95 V/us. A slewed step of height 2s loses the area (2s)^2 / (2 SR). Two edges per period give an offset of 4 f0 s^2 / SR in the demodulated mean. Subtracting its linear part from the 10 mV run gives the model D(A) = 4 f0 (0.975)^2 A (A - A10) / SR. With SR = 6.63 V/us this is 0.926 mV at 50 mV and 4.164 mV at 100 mV. The measured shortfall is 0.951 mV and 4.348 mV, so the model agrees to 3 % and 4 %. A fit of D(A) = k A (A - A10) + c (1 - A/A10) gives k = 0.315 mV/V^2 (model 0.297 mV/V^2) and c = 0.008 mV, so the error is quadratic in the amplitude. The constant delay of the switch (delta term of P-28) is linear in A and cancels in this comparison. The rail is not the cause: the same quadratic law holds at 50 mV, where X_DEM is 3 V from the rail.

The X edges fall at BPF_OUT = 0.223 A, and they step X_DEM upwards (rising rate). The same model gives +0.007 % in X_OUT at 100 mV. The measured +0.018 % is within the 0.011 % window dependence of the BPF_OUT reference. P-28 found the same slew term with an empirical rate of 7.60 V/us on rising edges. The datasheet gives 7.5 V/us rising and 5.5 V/us falling at ±18 V (SBOS869, page 8).

**Clipping.** The entry expected clipping at 100 mV on ±4.6 V, which are the rails of P-28. The bench rails of this circuit are +5VA = 5.12 V and -5VA = -5.13 V (P-23), inside the design-context target of 5.0 V to 5.2 V. On these rails the largest node is X_DEM at 4.03 V peak with a margin of 1.09 V, and no node clips. The threshold is estimated from the output limit of OPAx196.LIB (CLAWP and CLAWN tables). The drop from the rail is about 10.9 mV + 40.8 mV/mA sourcing and 7.6 mV + 42.3 mV/mA sinking. U3B (BPF_OUT) drives R72 (0.25 mA at 5 V), R78 and R83 (0.5 mA each when the switch connects GND) and C9, so about 1.25 mA at the peak. Its limit is then about 5.12 V - 0.062 V = 5.06 V, which BPF_OUT reaches at 5.06 V / 39.57 = 128 mV drain amplitude. X_DEM sinks about 0.53 mA through R79 and R14, so its limit is about -5.10 V. Its edge spikes of about 2 % touch this limit at about 126 mV drain amplitude, but they last less than 0.5 us. The first stage to clip as a sine is therefore U3B, at about 128 mV drain amplitude. This is an estimate from the model tables, not a simulated result.

**DC step.** The DC step is blocked by the K = 2 Sallen-Key high-pass (518 Hz, Q = 1). INA_OUT follows the step with the 10 ms R60/C74 time constant. The high-pass passes only the slope change at 50 ms, which gives a burst of 35.5 mV in HPF_OUT and 60.6 mV in BPF_OUT at 100 mV in the first 2 ms. After 60 ms the BPF_OUT difference is below 2 mV. The demodulated burst reaches the post-filter as a short impulse. Its area S gives the X_OUT and Y_OUT peaks within 7 % and the recovery times within 3 ms of the measured values. The remaining difference is the spread of the burst over a few ms and the lag of the trailing boxcar. The recovery time is therefore set by the post-filter time constant of 3 ms, not by R60/C74. With the board value cpf = 1.5 uF (0.3 s per pole) the same area gives a peak of 0.035 mV in X_OUT and 0.053 mV in Y_OUT at 100 mV, below the 0.1 mV tolerance.

**Cross-check.** The dstep = 0 run of P32b and the vda = 10 mV run of P32a are not the same netlist. The 0 V source V_DSTEP adds a node and a branch. The adaptive time grids therefore diverge from 16.7 ns, and the two runs place their samples differently at the switching edges. The maximum BPF_OUT difference of 2.6 mV and the X_DEM difference of 6.5 mV occur at 90 deg of the period, at the 12 mV BPF_OUT spike of the Y edges. The 99.9th percentile of the BPF_OUT difference is 0.16 mV and its median is 0.33 uV. The fitted BPF_OUT amplitudes differ by 0.49 uV (1.2 ppm). The difference is sampling of the edge spikes and not a circuit difference. It does not affect the P-32b difference, because the dstep runs and the dstep = 0 run share the same netlist.

**Start-up.** The drain bias starts at 0 V without an `.IC`, so the first 60 ms contain a start-up transient in BPF_OUT (about -411 mV at 66 ms, against -395 mV settled). The peaks in the clipping table therefore include this transient. The fits and the settled amplitudes use the last 20 periods.

## Open points

1. The clipping threshold of about 128 mV drain amplitude (U3B) is an estimate from the OPAx196 model tables. A run of `circuits/afe_eval_P32a.cir` with `.STEP PARAM vda LIST 120m 130m 140m` and the same probes plus `V(Y_DEM)` would confirm it.
2. Y_DEM is not probed. Its edge spikes are not measured. They are expected to match the X_DEM spikes.
3. At 100 mV the +IN of U26A and U27A (D1, D2, up to 3.95 V) passes the OPA2196 input transition region of 2.12 V to 3.62 V (P-23 rule). The model shows no effect beyond the slew term (c = 0.008 mV). The real part may show an offset step there. This is for the design review.
4. The slew error grows with the square of the quadrature component at the switch edges. At the board operating point it is a phase error of about 0.1 deg at 100 mV drain amplitude, far below the 1 deg threshold. A larger quadrature (other excitation phase) gives a larger error, up to 4 f0 A^2 / SR = 4.9 mV at 3.95 V pk with A fully in quadrature.
5. The recovery time is measured from the step at 50 ms, not from the end of the R60/C74 settling. The margin to the entry deadline is large, so the choice does not change the result.
