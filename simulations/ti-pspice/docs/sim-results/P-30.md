# P-30 Noise at X_OUT (SIM-16 for the board)

## Goal

Find the input-referred noise of the board and check that the amplifiers add at most 10 % of the noise power (AN-23 via LIA-Readout). Source: `simulations/simulation-index.md`, entry P-30.

## Method

**Pixel shot noise.** The shot noise of a pixel of current I is 2qI. A resistor of value R has the Norton current noise 4kT/R, so R = 2kT/(qI) gives the same noise. With kT/q = 0.025852 V at 300 K and I = 7.5 uA, R = 6.894 kOhm. The netlist expression `2*0.025852/7.5u` evaluates to this value. The entry text gave 6.894 MOhm, which is wrong by a factor of 1000. The main session has corrected the entry.

**Compensation.** At 6.894 kOhm the resistor draws 0.157 V / 6.894 kOhm = 23 uA from each drain. This is three times the 7.5 uA pixel current, and it would move the drain to about 3 mV. Its AC conductance would also reduce the drain impedance by about 98 %. A noise-free behavioural source cancels this load. `G_CSA PIX_D1 0 VALUE {-V(PIX_D1)/(2*0.025852/7.5u)}` and the same for PIX_D4 remove the DC and AC conductance of each resistor, so only its Norton noise current 2qI remains. This is the form approved by the main session.

**Temperature.** PSpice resistors use TEMP = 27 C, which is 300.15 K. The expected values use 300.15 K. At 300 K the values would be 0.025 % lower in amplitude.

**Drain referral.** The noise density at BPF_OUT is referred to the differential drain with the AC gain of the same run, G = V(BPF_OUT) / (V(DRN_B) - V(DRN_A)) = 39.5775 V/V at 518 Hz. Drain noise is the output density divided by |G|^2. The input-referred value relative to V_EXC printed in the `.out` (22.16 uV/rtHz) is not used, because V_EXC also drives the gate and the pixel modulation.

**Full contributor tables.** The `.out` lists every contributor of the `.NOISE` run at 518 Hz. These are the DIODE, RESISTOR and VOLTAGE CONTROLLED SWITCH tables, lines 2307 to 5630 of the `.out`, with 1641 items. The items sum to the TOTAL OUTPUT NOISE VOLTAGE within 0.004 % (ratio 1.00004). The `.out` does not truncate the list. The "Top Contributors" block at the end of the file repeats only the largest five items.

**Fold-down factor.** The factor is computed twice from the DEC run. Once as the white-input sum with |H(n f0)|, and once as the noise-weighted sum with the noise density S(n f0). Both use the odd harmonics up to 193 f0, with weights (4/(n pi))^2 / 2. The DEC grid has 50 points per decade, so the values at 518 Hz and at the harmonics come from log-log interpolation. The interpolated |H(f0)| is 39.5094 V/V against the exact 39.5775 V/V, a difference of 0.17 %. This error cancels in the ratio.

**Expected values.** The expected values come from `P-30_expected.py`. The drain is modelled with R_D = 330 kOhm, a node capacitance of 70 pF per side (cnode 50 pF, cmux 10 pF, INA828 C_CMN 10 pF) and C_DIFF of 1 pF between the drains. Each side has its own shot and R_D noise. The differential noise is computed from the full two-node admittance matrix. The fold-down factor and the post-filter ENBW are from the ideal chain.

## Amplifier terms

The largest amplifier term is the INA828 input current noise in the drain impedance. It gives 80.8 nV/rtHz, 1.20 % of the power. The current noise of each input acts on the drain impedance of 329 kOhm. Two inputs in quadrature give sqrt(2) x 57.1 nV = 80.8 nV/rtHz, so each input carries about 174 fA/rtHz. The INA828 current noise is not in the project documents and the datasheet is not verified. The model value is therefore the only source.

The excitation op amp U1B gives 64.2 nV/rtHz, 0.76 % of the power. Its noise reaches the drain through the pixel stand-in, which converts gate voltage into drain current with the sensitivity vda = 10 mV (an assumed value). The gate-to-drain gain is vda / vga = 10 mV / 2.5 mV = 4 V/V. This term therefore scales with the stand-in and is not a property of the board alone.

The INA828 voltage noise (XE) gives 9.1 nV/rtHz in the script group sum, or 8.8 nV/rtHz for the resistor items alone. The model value is larger than the 7 nV/rtHz quoted for P-15 in the request. The datasheet is not in the project, so the difference is not verified.

The RG resistor (R10, 5.62 kOhm) gives 8.68 nV/rtHz. This equals sqrt(4kT x 5.62 kOhm) x (G - 1) / G = 9.652 nV/rtHz x 0.8990 with G = 1 + 50 kOhm / 5.62 kOhm = 9.8968 V/V.

The entry expectation for the amplifier budget did not include the INA828 current noise multiplied by the drain impedance. This is the x R_D term that the entry omitted.

The VSUP row is the drain bias path. R60 and C74 give a gain of 0.0307 at 518 Hz. The U10A and U10B op amps give 0.52 nV/rtHz each in the drain-referred output. DAC2 and DAC3 are ideal sources in `afe_eval.cir`, so DAC noise on VSUP is not modelled.

## Discussion

**Sensor noise.** The sensor noise at the drain matches the corrected expectation to 0.00 %. The shot noise and the R_D noise are each within 0.1 % of their expected values. The entry value of 731.0 nV/rtHz is 0.27 % higher than the corrected value, because of the 70 pF node and the cross-coupling through C_DIFF. The 300.15 K temperature adds +0.025 %.

**Total and amplifier share.** The measured total is 736.78 nV/rtHz. Compared with sqrt(729.0^2 + amplifier terms^2), with the amplifier terms in quadrature (INA828 current, INA828 voltage, RG, U1B, R5, R7, R59, U10A, U10B and the others), the total matches to 0.00 %. The amplifier share is 2.10 % of the output power, which is well below the 10 % limit. The amplifier term of 106.8 nV/rtHz is below the budget of 243.0 nV/rtHz.

**Not modelled.** Pixel 1/f noise and the GMOS sensor model (TBD in `simulations/design-context.md`). The switch and demodulator noise, because the `.NOISE` analysis cannot include the switching. The DAC noise on the gate path and on VSUP, because the DAC sources are ideal. Everything after BPF_OUT: the demodulator, the post-filter and the X_OUT stage. The `.NOISE` analysis ends at BPF_OUT. The bias op amps U10A and U10B and the heater drivers are in the model, but their contributions are small.

## Open points

1. The entry text gave 6.894 MOhm. The main session has corrected it to 6.894 kOhm. The DC load and the drain impedance change described in the Method apply to the uncompensated circuit only, which was not used.
2. The pass criterion "within 2 % of the corrected expectation (sensor plus amplifier terms)" needs an expectation for the amplifier terms, which the entry does not give. The measured total of 736.78 nV/rtHz is compared with sqrt(729.0^2 + amplifier terms^2), where the amplifier terms are the simulated quadrature sum of 106.8 nV/rtHz. This comparison matches to 0.00 %. The judge should confirm that this is the intended criterion.
3. The INA828 input current noise and voltage noise are not verified against a datasheet. The project has no INA828 datasheet.
4. The X_OUT values are unrounded to 0.003 %. The -0.025 % difference to the expected 16.79 uV rms comes from the gain, 39.5872 V/V ideal against 39.5775 V/V simulated.
5. The superseded run P30a_noise518 used the uncompensated resistor. Its results are not used.
