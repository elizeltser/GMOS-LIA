# P-12 Driver output impedance at the demodulator input

## Goal

Measure the gain and phase error when the demodulator input node is driven through a series resistance (DI-06).

## Method

**Settled window.** The fast settings give 0.15 s of run time at 517 Hz. The settled window is the last 20 whole periods. It has a length of 38.6847 ms and runs from 111.3153 ms to 150.0000 ms. The window is measured from the run end, so it holds whole periods of both references.

**Mean.** The mean is the time-weighted trapezoid mean over the raw PSpice points, with the window ends interpolated. Point means are biased by the adaptive step (`docs/simulation-quirks.md`).

**Settling check.** The window mean is compared with the mean of the window of the same length before it, from 72.6306 ms to 111.3153 ms. The settling factor for tau = 10 kOhm x 1 uF = 10 ms is F = 3.706e-6. The corrected value is V_ss = (m - 2.5 V x F) / (1 - F).

**Theory.** The switch sends N00890 to D when SEL is low and REF when SEL is high (`vendor/README.md`). The X reference V2 is high in the first half period, so X is in its -1 state for (t mod T) < T/2. The Y reference is high from 482.6 us, so Y is in its -1 state for ((t - 482.6 us) mod T) < T/2. A channel in its -1 state draws (V(N00890) - 2.5 V) / 10 kOhm. With n channels in the -1 state and eps = rser / 10 kOhm, the node obeys

V(N00890) - 2.5 V = u / (1 + n eps), with u = Vin - Vref = 0.2 V x sin(2 pi x 517 Hz x t).

A channel output is 2.5 V + u k in its +1 state and 2.5 V - u k in its -1 state, with k = 1 / (1 + n eps). The period mean of the outputs gives the deltas. Two forms are computed. The exact form uses the 0.18 deg Y timing error (482.6 us). The closed form is the entry formula, which uses quarter-period edges.

**Node check.** The residual r = V(N00890) - Vin is compared with the ideal residual -u n eps / (1 + n eps) over the last period, away from the edges (10 us windows). The steps of r at each reference edge are the change of the time-weighted mean from [e - 3 us, e - 0.5 us] to [e + 0.5 us, e + 3 us]. The same windows are used for the theory.

**Pass rule.** As written in the entry, a delta passes when it is within the larger of 2 % of the entry value and 0.005 mV.

**Time step.** The maximum step of 5 us gives a VX gain error of 0.003 % at the fundamental (P-05). At 0.2 V this is a shift of about 2.8 uV in VX. It is the same for every rser, so it cancels in the deltas.

## Discussion

**VX.** The VX deltas match the exact theory within 0.002 mV at every rser. The largest difference is 0.00186 mV at 1 kOhm (0.017 %). The entry value of 0.1275 mV at 10 Ohm is 0.0004 mV above the closed form (0.12712 mV). This is a rounding or transcription difference in the entry, and it has no effect on the result.

**VY at 1 kOhm.** The entry expects -0.482 mV. The closed form gives this value, because it assumes quarter-period edges. The Y reference edges are 0.18 deg early (482.6 us against T/4 = 483.56 us). That timing error alone gives -0.448 mV in the exact theory. The measured value is -0.458 mV. The data support a larger Y edge delay than 0.18 deg. A delay of 235 ns (the total switching delay from P-01 and P-04) gives -0.456 mV, which agrees with the measurement to 0.002 mV. A constant lag of 126 ns alone gives -0.452 mV, which is 0.006 mV from the measurement. The 235 ns value includes a slew term that P-04 fitted at 2 V. Its amplitude dependence at 0.2 V was not checked, so this is a supported explanation, not a verified one. The residual at 100 Ohm supports the same conclusion. The measured -0.00349 mV is closer to the 235 ns theory (-0.00323 mV) than to the 0.18 deg theory (-0.00227 mV). Both lie within the 0.005 mV tolerance, so this is not a pass or fail point.

**Gain error and the design limit.** The X gain error at 10 Ohm is -0.0998 %. The entry expects -0.100 %. The Draft Major threshold in `simulations/design-context.md` section 7 is 0.1 % of the expected output. The 10 Ohm case sits at that threshold. The 100 Ohm case (-0.985 %) is about ten times the threshold, and the 1 kOhm case (-8.71 %) is about eighty-seven times. The severity is for the reviewer to decide. The data give the numbers.

**The eps and 2 eps steps.** The entry says that the node steps by eps or 2 eps times (Vin - Vref) at the quarter-period boundaries. The data show only the first case. The node drops by eps times A at both Y edges. The magnitude is eps times |Vin - Vref| at the edge, which is first order in eps. A 2 eps step needs two channels to switch together. The X and Y edges are 89.82 deg apart, so this never happens in this run. The X edges at 0 and 180 deg have u = 0, so they give no step. The quarter-period boundaries therefore carry the steps only at 90 deg and 270 deg (actually 89.82 deg and 269.82 deg).

**Second-order steps.** At 1 kOhm the first-order step (eps times A = 20 mV) is 9 % and 24 % higher than the measured steps (18.15 mV and 15.15 mV). The exact theory gives 18.18 mV and 15.15 mV. The 1 / (1 + n eps) factor accounts for this. Within the exact theory, the Y down step is 0.03 mV (0.2 %) below the measurement. This is not explained.

**Residuals.** The node model holds to 0.024 mV at 1 kOhm, which is about 0.1 % of the 20 mV step. The VX residual at 1 kOhm (0.0019 mV) is not explained. It is within the 2 % tolerance. The node charge injection (QINJ = 0.4 pC in the switch model) and the node capacitance are not in the ideal model. They may explain the residual, but this was not tested.

## Open points

1. The entry's VY expectation at 1 kOhm (-0.482 mV) uses quarter-period edges. It omits the 0.18 deg Y timing error, and a total Y delay of 235 ns gives -0.456 mV. The judge should decide whether the expected value should change. This analysis does not change it.
2. The entry's 10 Ohm VX value (0.1275 mV) is 0.0004 mV above the entry's own closed form (0.12712 mV). The judge should decide whether to correct it.
3. The entry names `circuits/lia_v3_fast.cir` with Vampl = 0.2 V. The run used `circuits/lia_v3_fast_P10a0.cir`, which has vampl = 0.2 V. The default file has vampl = 2. The entry should cite the copy. This was not changed.
4. The exact command line is not in a log. It is rebuilt from `sim-runs/P12_rser/P12_rser.cir` (not verified).
5. The entry says "2 eps" for the node steps. No 2 eps step occurs in this pattern (see Discussion).
6. The 235 ns Y delay was fitted at 2 V (P-01, P-04). Its amplitude dependence at 0.2 V was not checked.
7. The Draft Major threshold (0.1 % of the expected output, `simulations/design-context.md` section 7) is at the 10 Ohm gain error. The reviewer should decide the severity of DI-06 with these numbers.
8. The 2.5 V reference is an ideal source here (kref = 0). The demodulator reference of a real design (`simulations/design-context.md` section 2) was not modelled. Not verified.
9. The P-08 summary gives the prior window as 91.6306 ms to 111.3153 ms (19.68 ms long). The P-08 script uses 72.6306 ms to 111.3153 ms (38.68 ms, the same length as the window). This summary uses the latter. The P-08 text should be corrected. It was not changed here.
