# P-17 Square-wave excitation through the band-pass

## Goal

Check the calibration when the sensor signal is a square wave, whose harmonics pass the band-pass with a different gain and phase.

## Method

**Settled window.** The run has 0.15 s of simulated time. The window is the last 20 whole periods of 517 Hz, 38.6847 ms long, from 111.3153 ms to 150.0000 ms. The prior window of the same length is from 72.6306 ms to 111.3153 ms. The fast settings use 20 periods (`simulations/simulation-index.md`).

**Mean.** The mean is the time-weighted trapezoid mean of the raw PSpice points, with the window ends interpolated. This follows the point-mean quirk in `docs/simulation-quirks.md`.

**Settling check.** The last window is compared with the prior window. The model bias for a start at 2.5 V is F (2.5 V - V_ss), with F = 3.706e-6 for tau = 10 ms.

**Harmonic transfer.** A least-squares fit of a DC term and the 1f to 7f sinusoids of V(VSAMPLE) gives the complex amplitude of each harmonic. The input square has harmonic amplitudes 4A/(n pi) with A = 15 mV. Its phase follows the sine convention of P-09. The ratio of the VSAMPLE harmonic to the input harmonic is the measured H(n f). It is compared with the ideal H(n f) from the same chain model as P-09.

**Ideal sums.** The ideal VX and VY sums use all odd harmonics up to n = 3999:

- VX - 2.5 V = -(8A/pi^2) sum over odd n of |H(nf)| cos(phi_n) / n^2
- VY - 2.5 V = -(8A/pi^2) sum over odd n of |H(nf)| cos(phi_n + n w tau) / n^2, with tau = 482.6 us

The entry used tau = T/4 for VY. The script prints both values. The sums converge to 0.1 uV for n above 399.

**Sine calibration.** The sine run P17sin_sine uses the same chain and the same analysis. Its VX and VY are the calibration value for the square run.

## Discussion

**Calibration and the ratio.** The ratio of the square to the sine response is 1.25700. It matches the ideal value 1.25701 within 0.001 %. Both runs have the same chain. The common gain error of about -0.05 % cancels in the ratio. The ratio is therefore a direct check of the harmonic weights. It agrees with the ideal weights to 0.001 %.

**Size of the X error.** The square input changes VX by -1.276 % against the fundamental-only value. This is above the Draft major threshold of 0.1 % in `simulations/design-context.md` section 7. The harmonic effect on VX is +1.71 mV. The 3rd harmonic gives +1.41 mV of it, about 82 %. The fundamental alone gives -134.65 mV, and the sum over the odd harmonics gives -132.94 mV. A calibration that assumes the square is its fundamental reads the X output 1.28 % too large in magnitude. This is a design issue for the calibration, not for the simulation.

**Y timing.** The Y reference delay of 482.6 us gives an offset of 0.18 deg, which is a 0.96 us shift of the reference edge against T/4. The offset moves the square VY by -0.430 mV and the sine VY by -0.329 mV. The entry's VY value (+5.00 mV) uses T/4 and is therefore 0.43 mV too high for the present reference. The correct ideal value is +4.57 mV. The measured value of +4.33 mV differs from this by -0.24 mV.

**Amplifier bandwidth and time step.** The measured chain differs from the ideal chain by -0.054 % at 517 Hz and -0.125 deg. The deficit grows with the harmonic number. At 7f it is -0.14 % and -0.62 deg. Part of this may come from the 2 us step. The quirk in `docs/simulation-quirks.md` gives a 2 us error of 0.04 % at the 7th harmonic. The fundamental deficit does not change between 2 us and 5 us (P-09 gives -0.054 % and -0.126 deg at 5 us, and the 2 us sine run gives -0.057 % and -0.114 deg). The time step is therefore not the main cause of the fundamental deficit. The amplifier and switch models are the likely causes, as P-09 concludes. This is not verified.

**Effect on the calibration.** The amplifier deficit reduces the magnitude of VX by 0.05 %. This is below the 0.1 % threshold. The Y timing lowers VY by 0.43 mV, which is 8 % of the square VY value and is not small in the Y channel. The Y timing is a schematic issue (`V_V6` delay, see `simulations/simulation-index.md`, "Manual actions").

## Open points

1. The entry's VY expectation (+5.00 mV) assumes the Y reference at T/4. With the present 482.6 us delay the ideal value is +4.57 mV. The entry should be updated by the judge or the user. The analyst did not change the expected value.
2. The entry's R expectation (133.03 mV) should be 133.02 mV with the Y timing term. The difference is 0.01 mV and does not affect the result.
3. The +0.058 mV excess in the measured VY is not explained. The high harmonics (n of 9 and above) and the demodulator timing were not measured separately. A fit of the existing VSAMPLE data up to n = 15 would test whether the high harmonics account for it. The analyst did not run this fit (not verified).
4. The 0.05 % common gain deficit and the phase deficit of the chain are not identified. The OPAx192 and INA828 bandwidth and gain were not read from the models (not verified).
5. The sine run uses 2 us, and P-09 uses 5 us. The two results differ by 0.023 mV in VY. Future P-09 values for VY should use 2 us if they are compared with P-17.
