# P-11 Resistor mismatch with input offset

## Goal

Measure the DC and scale error from a mismatch of R1 against R2 in the X channel (D-05).

## Runs

Each run folder holds the circuit as run (`<run>.cir`), the text results (`<run>.csd`) and the log (`<run>.out`). Both ended with status `done`. The logs show only the known warnings. The analysis script is `docs/sim-results/scripts/P-11_mismatch.py`. Run it from the project root. It prints the tables below and writes `docs/sim-results/plots/P-11_mismatch.png`.

## Method

- **Settled window.** The last 20 whole periods of 517 Hz, from 111.3153 ms to 150.0000 ms (38.6847 ms). The window of the same length before it gives the settling check.
- **Means.** Time-weighted trapezoid means of the PSpice points, with the window ends interpolated. The settling bias is removed as in P-08. The correction is 0.4 uV to 0.5 uV.
- **Deltas.** Each step minus the dr1 = 0 step of the same run.
- **Expected values.** From the entry: delta VX = -(delta/2) x ost - (delta/2) x 2A/pi, with delta = dr1 and 2A/pi = 127.324 mV. Delta VY = 0.
- **Pass criterion.** The entry's criterion: the difference must be within the larger of 0.005 mV and 1 % of the expected value. Delta VY must be below 0.005 mV.
- **Interaction check.** The ost term at dr1 = 0 is subtracted from the ost = +0.3 V and ost = -0.3 V deltas. The rest must equal -0.3 x dr1 (mV), which is the expected part.
- **Mechanism check.** The period mean of V(VX_DEMOD) from the transient, against the entry ideal. It is split into a reference duty part and a switch delay part, using the 50 % points of V(N00261) and V(VX_DEMOD) in the second period. The window is T to 2T, with T = 1.934236 ms.

## Discussion

**The delta comparison passes.** The measured deltas match the entry formula within 0.0004 mV. Delta VY is zero within 0.0002 mV at every step. The mismatch sets the scale of VX as the entry predicts, and it does not touch VY.

**The ost term does not change the delta comparison.** The ost term of -0.153 mV/V is the same at every dr1. It cancels when each delta is taken against dr1 = 0 of the same run. The interaction check shows a residual of at most 0.0008 mV, which is 0.04 % of the delta. The answer to dispatcher note 2 is therefore no. The expected table is still valid.

**The cause is timing.** The dispatcher proposed a common-mode effect in the op amp, input current, or the switch on-resistance. The diagnostic runs rule out all three. The resistors in the netlist are ideal, so they cannot give an ost term at dr1 = 0 by themselves:

- An ideal op amp (VCVS of gain 1e6) keeps the slope at -0.1545 mV/V. The op amp model is not the cause.
- A switch on-resistance of 0.1 Ohm keeps the slope at -0.1525 mV/V. The on-resistance is not the cause.
- Removing the switch charge injection, capacitance and leakage keeps the slope at -0.1561 mV/V. Charge injection is not the cause.

The transient check locates the cause in the timing of the reference and the switch. The ost term has two parts.

1. **Reference duty (-0.104 mV/V).** The `PULSE` sources V_V2 and V_V6 define the width as `PW` measured from the end of the rise. The high time is therefore T/2 + t_slope, which is 967.218 us and not 967.118 us. The duty excess of 100 ns gives an offset of -2 x 100 ns / T = -0.1034 mV/V. The data give -0.1040 mV/V. In this case the sign of the ost term follows from the 100 ns excess, and the mechanism needs no switch model.
2. **Switch delay asymmetry (-0.048 mV/V).** The demodulator switches 41 ns after the rising reference edge and 87.5 ns after the falling edge. The difference of 46 ns gives -2 x 46.3 ns / T = -0.0479 mV/V. The data give -0.0483 mV/V. This part depends on the TMUX6136 behavioural model, which is not a TI model (`vendor/README.md`). The real delay of the TMUX6136 may differ.

Both parts scale with ost and not with the signal amplitude. That explains why P-03 at 2 V and P-11 at 0.2 V agree.

**Consequence for the P-03 open point.** The P-03 report gave the duty asymmetry of V2 and V6 as "about 0.1 mV" and called it too small for the p = 90 deg term. The present data give 0.104 mV per volt of ost for the X channel. This confirms the size. It does not explain the 15 mV p = 90 deg term of P-03, which stays open (not verified for p = 90 deg).

**VY has the same ost term.** The VY slope is -0.154 mV/V, about the same as VX. V_V6 uses the same `PULSE` definition, so the same duty offset is expected in the Y channel. This was not checked by a transient on the Y channel (not verified). The entry does not claim a VY ost dependence, so this is an extra finding.

**Limits.** The switch delays come from the behavioural TMUX6136 model, and the model's timing was not compared with the datasheet (not verified). The reference drive in the real design is TBD (`simulations/design-context.md` section 1). The duty part is a property of the test bench, and a drive with an exact 50 % duty would not give it. The switch part depends on the real part, which is not known.

## Open points

1. **Parameters line of P-11.** The index now gives the sorted list, `LIST -10m -1m 0 1m 10m`. The unsorted list failed (`P11p_mismatch.out.1`).
2. **Pulse definition of the reference sources.** The fix belongs in Capture and is the user's to make. A proposal is to set `PW` of V_V2 and V_V6 to `{0.5/f_osc - t_slope}`. The high time would then be T/2, and the falling edge would move 100 ns earlier. The reference would then be symmetric about T/4 + 50 ns, which adds a phase shift of 50 ns (0.009 deg at 517 Hz). The same change may apply to I_IEXC. The fix was not run. After it, the duty part should vanish. The switch delay part of -0.048 mV/V would stay, so the ost term would not go to zero.
3. **Expected values of P-10.** The Y reference delay of 482.6 us (V_V6) changes two P-10 expectations. See `docs/sim-results/P-10.md`.
4. **Model delay asymmetry.** The 46 ns asymmetry of the TMUX6136 model should be checked against the datasheet switching times (not verified). The check is needed before the switch delay part is used in the design.
5. **Settling residual of the two ost runs.** The window residual differs by about 5 uV between the two ost runs. The cause is not known. It does not affect the result.
6. **Dispatcher values.** The quoted VX values differ by 1 uV to 2 uV from the entry-window values. The window of the dispatcher was not stated.
7. **Diagnostic copies.** The three diagnostic circuits in `circuits/` are kept for reproduction. They are not part of the design. The 2 V diagnostic folders were overwritten (see Runs).
8. **Not verified.** The VY term (Y reference duty) was not checked by a transient on the Y channel. The P-03 p = 90 deg term is not explained. The entry's D-05 link was not checked against `simulations/design-issues.md`. The real TMUX6136 and OPAx192 timing and offset values were not compared with their datasheets.
