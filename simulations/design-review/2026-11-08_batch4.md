## 3. Findings

### F-01 SEL1 and SEL2 driven above VDD + 0.3 V before J4 is on (Critical)

- **Evidence.** TMUX6136 SCDS397B section 5.1 (quoted from the 2026-10-29 review F-05): digital pin voltage GND - 0.3 V to VDD + 0.3 V, current ±30 mA. SEL has a 6 MOhm pull-down and VIH 2 V min (section 5.5). In the board `REF_X` connects J7 pin 4, TP8 and U24 pin 1 only, `REF_Y` connects J7 pin 6, TP9 and U24 pin 9 only (P-31 summary, netlist of commit 16ad253). The owner's power rule fixes the order of J4 and J5. It does not fix when the NUCLEO timers start. A NUCLEO on USB runs its firmware before the bench supplies are switched on.
- **Simulated.** P-31: REF_X forward biases the SEL1 stand-in to +0.55 V, lifts the +15 V rail to 3.24 V in 1.25 ms and delivers 32 uC. The 2.45 A peak is the 1 Ohm placeholder of the stand-in and is not a board value.
- **Estimate.** `calc1`: with a 25 Ohm MCU output resistance (assumed) and a 0.6 V clamp drop, the pin carries (3.3 V - 0.6 V) / 25 Ohm = 108 mA. This is 3.6 times the 30 mA rating. The STM32F303 VOL and VOH and its output resistance are not verified (datasheet not read). The Critical severity does not depend on the 25 Ohm. The voltage rating (VDD + 0.3 V) is broken whenever VDD = 0 V and the pin is at 3.3 V. The current exceeds 30 mA for any source resistance below (3.3 V - 0.6 V) / 30 mA = 90 Ohm, and the same steady-state argument holds for any value, because only the first edges carry charge. The charge of 11.1 uF x 2.7 V = 30 uC (calc1) reproduces the P-31 value of 32 uC. The steady state is harmless because the rail floats up to 2.7 V and only edge charge flows. The damage risk is the first edges and the NUCLEO pin injection limit.
- **Consequence.** A part is outside its absolute maximum rating whenever the references run with J4 off. This includes every power-down in which J4 is switched off before the NUCLEO stops its timers.
- **Change.** Put 1 kOhm in series with `REF_X` and `REF_Y` at U24 pins 1 and 9, on the U24 side of TP8 and TP9. Calc1 gives:

| Series R | Peak clamp current | 30 mA limit | Rail time constant | SEL edge delay at 10 pF (to 2 V) | Phase of the delay at 518 Hz |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Ohm | 108 mA | exceeded | 0.28 ms | 0.2 ns | 0.0000 deg |
| 330 Ohm | 7.6 mA | ok | 3.9 ms | 3.3 ns | 0.0006 deg |
| 1 kOhm | 2.6 mA | ok | 11 ms | 9.5 ns | 0.0018 deg |
| 3.3 kOhm | 0.8 mA | ok | 37 ms | 31 ns | 0.0058 deg |

  The judge proposed about 3.3 kOhm. Both values protect the pin: 1 kOhm gives a peak 11 times below 30 mA, and 3.3 kOhm gives 37 times. 1 kOhm is chosen because it keeps the edge near the 10 ns of the references and matches F-05 of the 2026-10-29 review. The input capacitance of the SEL pin is CIN = 1.5 pF (SCDS397B section 5.5). The 10 pF in the table adds the test loop and the trace and is conservative, so the real delay is smaller than the table value. The delay is identical in X and Y, so it shifts only the common phase (calibrated) and the P-28 edge term (fitted 38 ns, section 5). A sequencing rule is still needed (section 6), because the series resistor does not make the pin legal. It keeps the current at a level the pin survives. With the rule (references off until J4 is on, off again before J4 goes off) the resistor is a second barrier.
- **Other NUCLEO lines (open question of P-31).** See section 4.1. They show no rating problem.
- **Test.** R-06 (simulation), bench check R-11 step 0.

### F-02 BPF_OUT drives S1A and S2A when the switch is unpowered (Major)

- **Evidence.** Analog pins Sx and Dx: VSS - 0.3 V to VDD + 0.3 V, ±30 mA (SCDS397B section 5.1, quoted). BPF_OUT is generated by U3B on ±5 V. The owner's order (J4 first, J5 second, reverse at power-down) keeps the switch powered. It does not protect against a trip or a pulled plug of the J4 supply while J5 is on, or against a hot-plug of J5 first.
- **Estimate.** `calc1`: with VDD = VSS = 0 V and a 10 Ohm op amp output impedance (assumed), BPF_OUT of 2 V pk gives 140 mA and 4 V pk gives 340 mA. The OPA2196 short-circuit current is ±30 mA at ±2.25 V and ±65 mA at ±18 V (SBOS869 section 6.5), so the real current is limited at about 30 mA to 65 mA, at or above the switch rating. At a drain amplitude of 10 mV, BPF_OUT is 0.4 V pk and does not forward bias the clamp (0.6 V).
- **Consequence.** Only a fault or a wrong order stresses the switch. Severity Major, not Critical, because the stated order avoids it.
- **Change.** 1 kOhm in series with `BPF_OUT` at U24 pins 2 and 10 (S1A, S2A). Calc1: peak 1.4 mA at 2 V, 3.4 mA at 4 V. The load of the switch is the high impedance +IN of U26A and U27A, so there is no DC drop. The extra delay of the +1 state is 14 ns (50 % point, 1 kOhm plus 120 Ohm RON, SCDS397B section 5.5, into 18.5 pF of which 5.5 pF is CD(ON) and the rest is assumed). It adds 0.0027 deg to the P-28 edge term and changes the fitted 38 ns to about 50 ns. This term is a quadrature error of the form 4 f0 delta A and is part of the Y zero of section 5. Because the resistor is in the signal path of the +1 state only, it must be the same in X and Y (two 1 % parts from one reel are enough).
- **Cost of the 1 kOhm (calc estimates).** DC drop: the leakage current of the closed switch ID(ON) is ±0.06 nA at 25 deg C and ±0.25 nA at 85 deg C (SCDS397B section 5.5), the op amp Ib is 20 pA maximum (SBOS869), and the series resistance is 1 kOhm + 120 Ohm = 1.12 kOhm. The drop is (0.25 nA + 0.02 nA) x 1.12 kOhm = 0.30 uV at +IN at most. With the noise gain of 2 this is 0.6 uV, below 1 uV at X_OUT, and the zero of section 5 removes it. Noise: the thermal noise of 1 kOhm is 4.07 nV/rtHz. Referred to the drain with the noise gain 2 of the +1 state, the only state in which the resistor is in the path, it is 2 x 4.07 nV / 39.57 = 0.2 nV/rtHz, and (0.2 / 729)^2 = 7.5e-8 of the 729 nV/rtHz power of P-30, below 1e-6. Loading: the divider of 1.12 kOhm against the +IN impedance (about 8 pF at 518 Hz, 38 MOhm, and 100 MOhm differential) is 3e-5, negligible.
- **Test.** R-06.

### F-04 Drain supply window against 7.5 uA through 330 kOhm (Major, conditional on the sensor model, TBD)

- **Evidence.** P-33c: the drain is VSUP_A - 7.5 uA x 330 kOhm = VSUP_A - 2.475 V. The index and LIA-Readout AN-9 give a VSUP_A window of 0.5 V to 3.5 V (not in `simulations/design-context.md`). P-33 states that the pixel stand-in is an ideal sink, so the model holds 7.5 uA at -1.98 V. The drain also passes through TMUX1109 (U20, U21) and the INA828 inputs. TMUX1109 absolute maximum for Sx and Dx is -0.5 V to VDD + 0.5 V, recommended 0 V to VDD (SCDS406A sections 5.1 and 5.3, read on 2026-11-08). VSS is on GND (U20 pin 3 and U21 pin 3 on GND, KiCad netlist exported by the judge on 2026-11-08).
- **Numbers (calc4).**

| VSUP_A | DAC2 | Drain at 7.5 uA | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.50 V | 0.46 V | -1.975 V open circuit, clamped at -0.40 V by the TMUX1109 ESD diode (estimate) | inside the -0.5 V absolute rating because of the clamp, but below the 0 V recommended value. The risk is to the pixel (negative drain) and to the linearity of INA_OUT, not to the TMUX1109 |
| 1.975 V | 1.80 V | -0.50 V | at the absolute rating without the clamp |
| 2.525 V | 2.30 V | +0.05 V | lower end of the 50 mV to 250 mV drain DC (Draft, `simulations/design-context.md` section 4) |
| 2.63 V | 2.40 V | +0.155 V | board default, P-23 |
| 3.50 V | 3.19 V | +1.025 V | ok |

- **Consequence.** About two thirds of the planned window puts the drain below ground. The ESD diode clamps the drain at about -0.4 V (estimate, a diode with 1 pA saturation current carrying 4.8 uA). The current is small because R_D limits it, so the TMUX1109 does not fail on current. The sensor drain sees a negative voltage, which is the risk to the GMOS pixel. The INA828 input range on ±5 V is -3 V to +3 V (DI-02 number, quoted) and is not the limit. At power-up DAC80508Z outputs sit at code 0, so VSUP_A = -0.1 x DAC6 = 0 V and the drain would be -2.475 V as soon as MUX_EN is high. MUX_EN has a pull-down (R38), so this needs a firmware error.
- **Change.** No schematic change. A firmware rule: DAC2 and DAC3 are set to at least 2.30 V (drain at least +50 mV at 7.5 uA) before MUX_EN is set, and are never commanded below 1.80 V. Update AN-4 and AN-9 for this board to the window VSUP_A = 2.5 V to 3.5 V, or state the pixel current at which 0.5 V applies. The owner decides the pixel current and compliance.
- **Test.** R-09.

### F-05 Heater driver headroom with the datasheet swing (Minor, Major if R-08 fails)

- **Evidence.** P-33a: the OPAx196 model needs 10.9 mV + 40.8 mV/mA below +5VP. The datasheet gives the output swing from the rail at RL = 2 kOhm as 60 mV typical and 500 mV maximum (SBOS869 section 6.8, read on 2026-11-08). The typical value is given for VS = 4.5 V to 8 V. The load current at the 60 mV point is therefore 1.095 mA at VS = 4.5 V and 1.97 mA at VS = 8 V, and the typical slope is 54.8 mV/mA to 30.5 mV/mA. The model gives 55.6 mV at 1.095 mA, so it represents the typical part at the low-supply end. The 500 mV maximum is specified only at about 1 mA to 2 mA. The datasheet gives no guaranteed value at 6.6 mA to 14 mA (figure 6, output swing against output current, not read). The board runs at VS = 10.2 V, above the typical range.
- **Numbers (calc2, revision 1, linear typical slope, 15 Ohm shunt, 4.5 Ohm switch, 40 uA divider current, +5VP 5.09 V and 4.97 V).**

| Heater | Model | Typical, 54.8 mV/mA | Typical, 30.5 mV/mA | Margin to 4.0 V, Draft 0.2 V |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 Ohm | 4.29 V / 4.19 V | 4.14 V / 4.04 V | 4.43 V / 4.32 V | 0.04 V to 0.43 V |
| 600 Ohm | 4.65 V / 4.54 V | 4.56 V / 4.45 V | 4.73 V / 4.62 V | 0.45 V to 0.73 V |
| 1200 Ohm | 4.85 V / 4.74 V | 4.81 V / 4.69 V | 4.90 V / 4.79 V | 0.69 V to 0.90 V |

  Each cell gives the HFB_A ceiling at +5VP 5.09 V / 4.97 V. +5VP is within 25 mV of +5VA because the owner sets J5 for +5VA = 5.0 V to 5.2 V at TP3 and R32 and R18 are both 1 Ohm. The B5819W model dependence is therefore small: a 14 mA step changes the diode drop by 6 mV (r_d about 0.45 Ohm at 60 mA, estimate). The worst case for the headroom is +5VA at 5.00 V.
- **Severity.** A margin below the Draft 0.2 V is Minor by the levels of `simulations/design-context.md`. It would be Major only if the swing could fail at the heater current. The datasheet does not guarantee it at 6.6 mA to 14 mA, so the typical-part ceiling is an expectation and not a limit. R-08 measures it. If the ceiling on the heater actually used is below 4.0 V, the finding becomes Major.
- **Consequence.** A 600 Ohm heater cannot reach the model value of 4.6 V (44 mV net headroom) on a typical part at the 4.5 V end of the slope range. A 300 Ohm heater (the figure in the experiment notes, which conflicts with AN-16) has 0.04 V to 0.43 V of margin at 4.0 V and its current of 13.1 mA clips HSENSE_B at 3.3 V (8.8 mA limit, confirmed by P-33a, F-09 of 2026-10-29).
- **Change.** State the heater resistance and the heater voltage target (TBD in the board documents). For 300 Ohm: either limit the command to HFB_A = 3.8 V or add the shunt change of F-09 (R24, R30 to 7.5 Ohm halves the HSENSE gain and the clip current doubles to 17.6 mA at the cost of 2 times the INA190 offset). For 600 Ohm and above no change.
- **Test.** R-08 (bench). A simulation of the swing is not possible with the model (R-07).

### F-06 Heater loop damping (Minor)

- **Evidence.** P-33b: overshoot 1.8 % at 100 pF, 22.5 % at 1 nF (370 kHz ring), 58.4 % at 10 nF (122 kHz ring, zeta 0.17). The cause is the 1.47 kOhm open-loop output impedance of the model with the load capacitance. The datasheet value is ZO = 700 Ohm at 1 MHz (SBOS869 section 6.5, figure 19 not read), half of the model value. The hand loop model of `calc2` (single pole, ZO, shunt, switch, load) gives 46 deg to 59 deg at 10 nF, against 19 deg from P-33b, so it does not represent the model and its phase margins are not used.
- **Consequence.** The ring decays and the heater is resistive with a thermal time constant of seconds. A cable of 1 nF is a stress case for a socket board. The risk is an undamped ring with a longer cable or a capacitive heater lead. This is below the Major level. The rating of the earlier review (F-07, 40 deg to 50 deg estimated) was too optimistic for the model.
- **Change.** Add DNP 0603 C0G pads in parallel with R20 and R26 (feedback resistors), populate 22 pF if the heater leads exceed 0.5 m. Add DNP pads for a series isolation resistor of 10 Ohm to 22 Ohm between HFB_A and the TMUX1109 DB pin only if R-07 shows that it helps (the hand model could not decide).
- **Test.** R-07.

### F-07 Reference edges can couple into the drain nodes (Minor)

- **Mechanism.** REF_X and REF_Y are 3.3 V edges of 10 ns next to U24. A stray capacitance Cs from a reference trace to a drain node (330 kOhm, 70 pF) injects a step Cs/(Cs + 70 pF) x 3.3 V that decays with 23 us. The repetition at the reference frequency is coherent, so averaging does not remove it.
- **Estimate (assumed Cs).** The fundamental of the pulse train is 4 dV tau f0. For Cs = 0.01 pF: dV = 0.47 mV, tau = 23 us, fundamental 22 uV pk at the drain. At X_OUT this is at most (2/pi) x 39.6 x 22 uV = 0.56 mV, which is 0.22 % of the 10 mV signal. Cs = 0.1 pF gives ten times that. This is an upper bound with the whole fundamental in the measured phase. The simulations cannot show it because they contain no stray capacitance.
- **Change.** Layout rule: REF_X, REF_Y, SPI and EXC_DAC stay at least 5 mm from DRN_A, DRN_B and the TMUX1109 pins, with a ground guard between them. The zero of section 5 (references running, calibration position) measures and removes the constant part. Changes that depend on the excitation amplitude are not removed.

### F-08 Sallen-Key feedthrough with the datasheet output impedance (Minor)

- **Evidence.** P-34: the stopband of the post-filter is set by the closed-loop output impedance of U26B and U27B. The model gives L = 81.6 uH, a floor of 2.56e-9 f and a 2f0 ripple of 453 nV. A comb of 350 nV per line carries 10.4 uV rms at 0.4 V.
- **Estimate (calc4).** The follower impedance is ZO f / GBW with ZO = 700 Ohm (datasheet) and GBW 2.2 MHz to 2.5 MHz, so L = 44.6 uH to 50.6 uH. The floor becomes 1.4e-9 f to 1.6e-9 f, the 2f0 ripple 247 nV to 281 nV, the 4f0 ripple 98 nV to 112 nV and the comb about 5.7 uV rms. Scaling the model by the ZO ratio 700 / 1470 gives 216 nV. The range is 0.22 uV to 0.28 uV. Each case exceeds the 0.1 uV check of the index. It is 1.3 % to 1.7 % of the 16.96 uV rms noise of X_OUT and 8.6 nV to 11.2 nV at the drain (216 nV to 281 nV divided by 25.19 V/V).
- **Consequence.** No effect on a DMM reading or on a scope at 1 mV/div. The 2f0 limit of SIM-15 (40 dB) passes by more than 80 dB. The ripple matters only if an ADC later samples X_OUT (LIA-Readout) and aliases the comb. The 0.1 uV figure has no source in `simulations/design-context.md` (performance targets TBD).
- **Change.** None for the evaluation board. For a board with an ADC use R14 = R15 = 1 MOhm and C13 = C15 = 300 nF (same tau, feedthrough five times lower, bias error 5 pA x 2 MOhm = 10 uV, leakage see F-09).
- **Test.** R-13.

### F-09 Post-filter capacitors (Minor)

- **Evidence.** Component selection: C13 to C16 are Rubycon 16MU155MA23216, 1.5 uF, ±20 %, 16 V, acrylic film. Insulation resistance, dielectric absorption and voltage coefficient are not in the project documents (not verified).
- **Estimate (calc3).** The DC gain error of the unity-gain stage is -(R14 + R15) / R_IR. 100 Mohm gives -0.40 %, 400 Mohm -0.10 % (the Draft threshold), 1 Gohm -0.04 %. X and Y have separate capacitors, so the error is also an X/Y mismatch. The 20 % tolerance changes tau, not the DC gain, and sets the settling time (2.3 s for 1 mV at 245 mV, A-09) and the 2f0 attenuation.
- **Change.** Read the capacitor datasheet for the insulation resistance and absorption. Measure with R-12 before the first calibration. If R_IR is below 400 Mohm use a PPS film capacitor of 1.5 uF or two 0.75 uF C0G/film parts.

### F-10 Margins at high amplitude (Note)

- BPF_OUT clips at 117 mV drain (datasheet maximum swing 500 mV at 2 kOhm, 4.62 V) to 128 mV (typical 60 mV swing, 5.06 V), calc4. P-32 estimated 128 mV. The linear range requirement is 50 mV, so the margin is 2.3 to 2.6 times.
- The demodulator inputs pass the OPA2196 transition region (2.12 V to 3.62 V on 5.12 V) from 53 mV to 91 mV drain amplitude. SBOS869 gives Vos ±25 uV typical and ±100 uV maximum, and a 0.1 Hz to 10 Hz noise of 1.4 uVpp below and 7 uVpp above the region. The offset change of a stage in the region is at most 0.2 mV at X_OUT for the stage weights 2 and 1, only during part of the cycle. This is 0.016 % of the 1.26 V at 50 mV drain. The model has no step in the region (P-28, P-33c), so a bench check is needed (R-11 step 5).
- The phase rotation of 0.10 deg at 100 mV (P-32, U27A falling slew, datasheet 5.5 V/us falling and 7.5 V/us rising) is below 1 deg and below 0.1 % of R. It is a quadrature term and moves Y by up to 0.78 %. Y is therefore not a reliable amplitude channel above 50 mV. Use R.

## 5. Practical zeroing of offset, phase and gain (A-06, A-07, A-08)

The owner asked for a bench calibration that removes the offset, phase and gain mismatch between the channels. The method uses only what the board has: the references, the NUCLEO excitation phase, the calibration position of the TMUX1109, TP15 and TP16, and a 6.5 digit DMM on X_OUT and Y_OUT. Numbers come from `calc3`.

### 5.1 Principle

The excitation phase `p` is stepped by the NUCLEO in equal steps over 360 deg, as in P-27. The references stay fixed. At each step X_OUT and Y_OUT settle. The pairs (X, Y) trace an ellipse:

X = X0 + a cos(p + theta), Y = Y0 + b sin(p + theta + eps)

| Fit parameter | Meaning | Removes |
|---|---|---|
| X0, Y0 | Centre of the ellipse | Offsets that do not rotate with the excitation phase: Vos, bias current, reference pickup, switch charge. Excitation-synchronous pickup rotates with p and is not in the centre |
| b / a | Axis ratio | X/Y gain mismatch |
| eps | Angle between the axes minus 90 deg. It is removed with the formula of step 6, a rotation alone does not remove it | X/Y orthogonality |
| a (with b/a) | Amplitude | Needs a known signal for an absolute value (step 4) |
| theta | Phase | Needs a known signal for an absolute value (step 4) |

The centre is independent of the unknown sensor phase and amplitude. The sensor itself can be the signal at 10 mV to 50 mV drain. The fit is linear (least squares on 1, cos p, sin p).

### 5.2 Precision of the fit (calc3, Monte Carlo of 2000 fits)

The reading noise is 16.96 uV rms with a correlation time of 1.2 s (ENBW 0.417 Hz, P-30). The post-filter needs 4.6 s to settle to 4e-6 of a step (x = 15.2 time constants of 0.3 s).

| Points | Dwell per point | Time | s(X0) and s(Y0) | s(b/a) | s(eps) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 10 s | 2 min | 2.1 uV | 16 ppm | 0.0009 deg |
| 8 | 20 s | 3.3 min | 1.5 uV | 12 ppm | 0.0007 deg |
| 16 | 30 s | 9.3 min | 0.9 uV | 7 ppm | 0.0004 deg |

The 8-point sweep at 10 s resolves the largest uncalibrated terms of F-03 by factors of 400 (offset 0.91 mV to 2.1 uV), 120 (0.2 % to 16 ppm) and 60 (0.057 deg to 0.0009 deg). The sweep uses 0.25 V amplitude (10 mV drain). Lower amplitudes do not change the precision of the centre, only that of the ratio and angle.

### 5.3 Procedure

| Step | Action | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Power | Switch J4 (±15 V) on first with the NUCLEO references stopped (firmware flag) or unplugged from J7, then J5. Set J5 so that TP3 reads 5.00 V to 5.20 V. Start the references last. Power down in the reverse order | TP3 in range, no rail drops on the ±15 V (F-01, F-02) |
| 1 Warm-up and DC | 30 min. Check GATE (0.995 V), VSUP_A and VSUP_B, INA_OUT with the calibration position, heater off. Set DAC2 and DAC3 to give a drain of +0.1 V to +0.2 V at the pixel current (F-04) | Within 2 mV of the values in P-23 |
| 2 Zero reading (quick) | Select the calibration position (R_CAL) on both sides with the references and the excitation running. Wait 12 s, average 60 s. Record X_cal0, Y_cal0 | This is the offset plus the excitation-synchronous pickup. It is the zero for a measurement with a sensor at the same excitation |
| 3 Phase sweep on the sensor or the dummy card | Eight excitation phases, 45 deg apart, settle 5 s, average 10 s. Fit the ellipse | Centre within 2 uV of the zero of step 2 if there is no excitation-synchronous pickup. The difference between the two is the pickup (F-07) |
| 4 Absolute gain (once per board and again after rework) | Set MUX_EN low (U20 and U21 open, sensor not connected to the drains) and set VSUP_A and VSUP_B equal within 20 mV. The drain node is then R_D to VSUP, and the injection through R90 (100 kOhm) gives the divider 330 / (100 + 330) = 0.767. Connect the generator to TP15 in series with a 100 nF film capacitor (AC coupling, 3.07 kOhm at 518 Hz, lead 0.41 deg against the 430 kOhm). As an alternative, set the generator DC offset to the measured VSUP_A. A DC-coupled generator at 0 V would pull DRN_A to 100 / 430 x VSUP_A = 0.61 V at 2.63 V, the difference to DRN_B would be about 2 V, and the INA828 would saturate. Use a generator at f0 + 0.02 Hz, 13 mV pk (about 10 mV pk at DRN_A, 0.767 x 13 mV = 9.97 mV). The NUCLEO excitation is off during the beat measurement: EXC_DAC is held at its DC level, the references keep running. Measure the generator amplitude at TP15 with the DMM in AC mode (the accuracy at 13 mV and 518 Hz is the specification of the instrument used, not verified). Record X and Y over three beats (150 s). The beat amplitude is divided by the post-filter gain at 0.02 Hz (0.9986, tau 0.3 s, ±0.06 % for 20 % tau). The result is the gain from the drain node to X_OUT, expected 25.19 V/V (P-27). The beat phase between X and Y is 90 deg + eps and cross-checks step 3. The calibration position (R54 = 18 kOhm to ground) gives 0.146 instead of 0.767, so 13 mV would give 1.9 mV at the drain. A fitted sensor loads the node with an unknown impedance, so this step is done without the sensor | Gain within the limits of P-25 (±14 % for 5 % C0G, ±2.6 % for 1 %). Store G_cal. Accuracy: R_D and R90 at 0.1 % give a divider error of (1 - 0.767) x 0.2 % = 0.05 %, the post-filter correction adds 0.06 %, together 0.08 % (RSS). The amplitude meter adds its own specification (not verified) |
| 5 Checks | Linearity at 10, 30, 50 and 90 mV drain (R / amplitude constant to 0.03 %, transition region F-10). Supply ripple: 20 mV at 518 Hz on +5VA added by a transformer or a source, X_OUT shift below 10 uV | Linear to 0.05 %. Ripple response recorded for DI-26 |
| 6 Apply | Correct the readings with a = amplitude of X and b = amplitude of Y from the fit: X' = X - X0 and Y'' = ((Y - Y0) a / b - X' sin eps) / cos eps. With X = X0 + a cos u and Y = Y0 + b sin(u + eps) this gives X' = a cos u and Y'' = a sin u (check: a sin(u + eps) = a sin u cos eps + a cos u sin eps). Report R = sqrt(X'^2 + Y''^2) / G_cal | R is unchanged by a rotation of the pair. The removal of eps matters for the angle |

An optional dummy card for the sensor socket gives an absolute phase: a resistor Rg = 82.5 kOhm in series with 100 nF C0G from GATE to one drain pin (side A only) gives 30.3 nA pk and about 10 mV pk across 330 kOhm. The 100 nF blocks the 12.1 uA DC that Rg alone would inject. The series capacitor adds a known lead of 2.13 deg. The result fixes the phase of the whole chain from the NUCLEO excitation to X_OUT, including the MFB, the drain node and the band-pass. Without it the absolute phase of a sensor is TBD anyway, because the sensor model is TBD. It is not needed for R.

- **Schematic:** `circuits/afe_eval_R06.cir`, a copy of `afe_eval_P31.cir` with `R_SELX KEX_PIN REF_X_S 1k`, the same for Y, 1 kOhm between BPF_OUT and the S1A and S2A pins, a 25 Ohm source resistance in V_REFX and V_REFY, the stand-ins with the area factor `1e-6`, and V_DAC0 to V_DAC7 gated by a ramp that follows P5VA (a `VALUE` source with `limit`).

- **Expected:** peak SEL current (3.3 - 0.55) / (1 kOhm + 25 Ohm) = 2.7 mA (calc1: 2.6 mA with 0.6 V). The +15 V rail reaches 2.7 V with a time constant of 11.4 ms, so about 1.6 V at 10 ms before J4. The fault case (c): BPF_OUT at 1.98 V gives (1.98 - 0.55) / 1.01 kOhm = 1.4 mA, and 3.4 mA at the 4 V of 100 mV drain. The edge delay is 9.5 ns at SEL and 14 ns at the S pins, so X_OUT and Y_OUT final values differ from P-27 by less than 0.2 mV (delta term 4 f0 A delta: 4 x 518 x 2 V x 12 ns = 0.05 mV at A = 2 V, 5 uV at 0.2 V). Pass: all pin currents below 3 mA, forward conduction only in the first 40 ms, final values within 0.2 mV of P-27.
- **Status:** planned.

### R-07 Heater loop compensation

- **Goal:** find the smallest change that gives at most 10 % overshoot at 1 nF and 25 % at 10 nF, and measure the phase margin with `.AC`.
- **Schematic:** `circuits/afe_eval_P33b.cir` copies: (a) Cf across R20 of 10, 22 and 47 pF, (b) Rs of 10 and 22 Ohm between HFB_A and the switch (outside the feedback), (c) U9A from the OPAx192 model of `vendor/sbom862f/`. Loop-gain copy: the feedback broken at R20 with a 1 GH and 1 GF pair.
- **Parameters:** transient as P-33b (0.1 V step on DAC1, `.STEP chtr LIST 100p 1n 10n`, 50 ns step). `.AC DEC 50 1k 10Meg` for the loop gain, probes `V(HFB_A)` and the loop node.
- **Expected:** the baseline reproduces P-33b (1.8 %, 22.5 %, 58.4 % within 1 point) and gives about 19 deg phase margin at 10 nF. No value is predicted for the options: the hand model of calc2 gave 46 deg to 59 deg and failed to reproduce the baseline. A zero at 1/(2 pi R20 Cf) is 319 kHz, 145 kHz and 68 kHz for the three Cf, next to the observed rings of 370 kHz and 122 kHz, which is why they are tested. A smaller Zo is not testable with the black-box model, so the result is an upper bound on the ring. Pass: the baseline reproduces P-33b within 1 point. The best option gives overshoot of at most 10 % at 1 nF and at most 25 % at 10 nF, and a phase margin of at least 55 deg at 1 nF and at least 40 deg at 10 nF (the overshoot limits correspond to zeta 0.59 and 0.40, phase margin about 59 deg and 43 deg). The `.AC` phase margin agrees with the transient overshoot within 10 deg.
- **Status:** planned.

### R-08 Heater swing on the bench

- **Goal:** measure the real heater output swing and the headroom with 300 Ohm and 600 Ohm dummy heaters.

### R-09 Drain compliance and the TMUX1109 clamp

- **Goal:** show the drain voltage and the INA_OUT behaviour when VSUP_A is swept through the range 0.5 V to 3.5 V at 7.5 uA.

- **Expected:** DRN_A = 1.1 x vdrc_a - 7.5 mV - 2.475 V for vdrc_a above about 1.99 V (drain above -0.3 V). ### R-10 Phase-sweep calibration on simulated data

### R-11 Calibration procedure on the board

- **Goal:** run section 5 on the first board and record the residuals.
- **Schematic:** the board with the sequence of step 0, DMM 6.5 digits on X_OUT and Y_OUT through 10 MOhm, generator on TP15.
- **Parameters:** steps 0 to 6 of section 5.3. Repeat step 2 four times over 2 h at constant temperature, and once with a 10 K change (hair dryer on the board, logging TP temperature).
- **Expected:** step 2 and step 3 centres agree within 5 uV if there is no excitation-synchronous pickup. Repeatability of step 2 is 2 uV to 6 uV (calc3 for 10 s). Offsets between 0 and 0.5 mV (RSS of Vos at the maximum 0.5 mV). Step 4 gain within the P-25 limits. Linearity to 0.05 % up to 90 mV. Supply ripple response below 10 uV for 20 mV at 518 Hz. The drift over 10 K is at most 45 uV (4.5 uV/K worst case). Pass: (1) the four repeats of step 2 agree within 6 uV (1 sigma, calc3 for 10 s averaging). (2) the sweep centre of step 3 differs from the step 2 zero by at most 5 uV, or the difference is explained as excitation-synchronous pickup. (3) the fit residual is at most 10 uV rms. (4) b / a is within 0.2 % of 1 and |eps| is at most 0.05 deg. (5) the step 4 gain is within the P-25 limits and the X/Y beat phase is 90 deg ± 0.05 deg and agrees with 90 deg + eps of step 3 within 0.01 deg. (6) R / amplitude is constant to 0.05 % from 10 mV to 90 mV. (7) a 20 mV ripple at 518 Hz on +5VA moves X_OUT by less than 10 uV. (8) the offset change over 10 K is at most 45 uV.
- **Status:** planned (bench).

### R-12 Post-filter capacitor leakage and settling

- **Goal:** measure the insulation resistance of C13 to C16 through the DC gain of the post-filter.
- **Schematic:** the board, DMM on X_DEM (demodulator output test loop) and X_OUT, with a DC-like X value (excitation at 10 mV drain and p = 0).
- **Parameters:** wait 12 s, 10 readings each of X_DEM and X_OUT with the same meter on the same range. Repeat for Y. Then a step of excitation phase and 20 min of X_OUT logging after it.
- **Expected:** X_OUT = X_DEM x (1 - 400 kOhm / R_IR). For R_IR of 1 GOhm the difference is 0.04 % (100 uV at 245 mV), for 400 MOhm 0.10 % (245 uV). Meter accuracy 0.002 % allows 5 uV.
